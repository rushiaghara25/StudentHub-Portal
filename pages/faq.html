Find answers to common questions about using StudentHub.

How do I register for StudentHub?

Open the Register page, enter your student information, and submit the registration form.

How do I login?

Use your registered email address and password on the Login page.

Where can I find my student information?

After login, open the Profile page from the Dashboard.

How do I find college events?

Open Events from the student navigation or Dashboard.

How can I register for an event?

Open an event, read its details, and use the event registration option. Students should login before registering.

What if I still need help?

Visit the Contact page to get StudentHub support information.
