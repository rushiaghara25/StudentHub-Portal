Frequently Asked Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online student portal where students can register, log in, view events, manage their profiles, and receive important college announcements.

How can I create an account?

Go to the Register page, fill in your personal details, create a password, and submit the registration form.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Click the "Forgot Password" option on the Login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

How can I participate in college events?

Visit the Events page, explore the available events, and complete the registration process for the event you wish to attend.

Can I update my profile information?

Yes. After logging in, you can update your contact details, profile picture, and other personal information.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes. StudentHub is designed to keep student information safe and accessible only to authorized users.

How can I contact StudentHub support?

Visit the Contact page and use the provided email, phone number, or contact form to reach our support team.

can I give feedback?

Open the Feedback page, write your suggestions or comments, and submit the form. We appreciate every suggestion.
